# Gilgamesh – Dock Scene Prototype

A point-and-click prototype built as a single self-contained HTML page (all art and audio are embedded).

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser (or via GitHub Pages).

## Controls
- Click the dock to walk.
- Click the door on the left house to knock (each knock turns on a light; keep knocking…).
- Click the handle on the floor to pick it up; it goes into the inventory bar.
- Select the handle in the inventory, then click the robot on the right to throw it to him.
- After that, click the robot on the right and Gilgamesh points at him.
- Bottom bar: walking-line debug, sound on/off, music and ambience volume.

Browsers only allow sound after the first click on the page.

## Audio
The game has all its sounds built into `index.html`, so it works on its own.
The original sound files are also kept in `audio/` for editing:
footsteps (4), door knocks (3), harbor ambience, music, jaw snap, pointing voice line, handle pickup, handle fall and throw.
